2. Information We Collect

2.1 Data stored locally on your device. ReflectIQ is designed to keep your personal data on your own device. The interaction entries you create (including titles, client names, interaction types and outcomes, confidence and energy levels, and any free-text notes) are stored only in a local database on your device. This data is not uploaded to our servers and we do not have access to it.

2.2 Subscription and purchase data. The App uses RevenueCat to manage in-app subscriptions and purchases. When you make a purchase, RevenueCat and the app store operator (Apple or Google) process your transaction. This may include an anonymous, randomly generated app user ID and purchase/entitlement status. We do not receive your payment card details; payments are processed entirely by Apple App Store or Google Play.

2.3 Analytics and diagnostics. RevenueCat may collect limited technical and diagnostic information (e.g., device type, OS version, app version) in order to provide and secure the subscription service. We do not use third-party advertising networks and we do not sell your data.

2.4 App preferences. Simple settings and flags are stored locally on your device.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide the core functionality of the App (storing and displaying your locally saved interaction records);
  • To manage, verify and restore your premium subscription or purchases;
  • To deliver app updates and support;
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share data only with the following service providers, solely as needed to operate the App:

  • RevenueCat — subscription management (RevenueCat Privacy Policy);
  • Apple App Store / Google Play — payment processing and app distribution.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your interaction data is stored locally until you delete it within the App or uninstall the App, at which point the local data is removed from your device. If you request deletion of your RevenueCat-associated user data, contact us at the email below and we will process your request.

7. Children's Privacy

8. Security
